The problem

Small teams can't answer every call and keep every chair full.

Questions pile up

The same questions about prices, hours and policies, while you're with a client.

Customers wait

A missed call or a slow reply, and the booking goes somewhere else.

Slots go empty

A last-minute cancellation, and someone who wanted that time never hears about it.

How it works

Set up once. It works for you every day.

1

Upload your documents

Price lists, service menus, policies, FAQs, even photos of your price board. SmartDesk reads them as they are and checks they're safe for your customers before anything goes live.

2

It answers your customers

On your website and by phone, about services, prices and policies, using only your documents. When they don't say, it hands over to your staff.

3

It handles bookings

Customers check availability, book, reschedule or cancel in one conversation. Anything it can't handle goes to your staff, with the reason.

Plus

The empty chair gets filled.

When an appointment is cancelled, SmartDesk can offer the opening to customers on your waitlist who asked to hear about one. Nothing is booked until they accept.
