SmartDesk

An AI front desk for appointment-based businesses

Your front desk, always open: answering, booking, and keeping your calendar full.

Small spas and salons juggle calls, repeat questions, bookings and last-minute changes, often with no one free to answer. Customers wait. Appointments slip. Open slots go unfilled.

What SmartDesk does

1

Learns your business, with your approval

You share service details, policies, documents and past support call recordings you're authorized to use. SmartDesk transcribes, cleans and organizes them, flags gaps and conflicts, and asks you to resolve them. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

2

Answers your customers

A chat assistant on your website and a phone line answer questions about services, prices and policies, using only your approved knowledge.

3

Handles booking from start to finish

Customers can check availability, book, reschedule or cancel in the same conversation. Anything it can't handle is passed to your staff, with the reason attached.

Plus: fills cancelled slots

When an appointment is cancelled, SmartDesk can offer the opening to eligible customers on your waitlist who opted in. No booking is confirmed until the customer accepts.

Built on trust

  • Answers come only from knowledge you've approved
  • Call recordings only suggest knowledge; you decide what's true
  • Personal details are hidden before anything is stored
  • Customers opt in to waitlist offers and accept before they're booked

Initial scope

For appointment-based businesses, starting with spas and salons:

  • Business knowledge from documents and call recordings, owner-approved
  • Website customer assistant
  • Phone support
  • Full booking: check availability, book, reschedule, cancel
  • Hand-off to staff
  • Filling cancelled slots from the waitlist

Planned for later

  • Campaign management
  • More customer engagement workflows

Better operations for the business. A better experience for the customer.

SmartDesk is a proposed product in concept stage. Features described are planned, not yet available.
